Institutional Overview

About StudyHub

An independent, open discovery index built to help Indian students find authoritative, verified learning materials from original educational authorities.

Our Purpose

Indian students navigating school board examinations and national entrance tests frequently encounter search results cluttered with aggressive advertising, outdated syllabi, paywalled portals, and low-quality re-uploads.

StudyHub was created with a clear objective: to provide a clean, dignified, and fast directory of verified educational links. We connect students directly to original, authoritative sources such as NCERT, CBSE Academic, NTA, and UPSC without requiring sign-ups, subscriptions, or invasive tracking.

Who We Serve

The StudyHub platform is tailored specifically for:

  • School Students (Classes 1 to 12): Preparing for CBSE, ICSE, and state secondary and senior secondary board assessments.
  • Competitive Exam Aspirants: Preparing for NEET UG (Medical), JEE Main & Advanced (Engineering), CUET UG (Central Universities), and UPSC Civil Services.
  • Government Employment Candidates: Preparing for Staff Selection Commission (SSC CGL), Railway Recruitment (RRB NTPC), and Banking examinations (IBPS PO).
  • Educators and Parents: Seeking verified curriculum blueprints and official exemplar problem sets for teaching.

Our Curation & Discovery Principles

  1. Direct Official Redirection: We do not rehost or redistribute third-party copyrighted textbooks. Every resource link points directly to the publisher's official server (e.g., ncert.nic.in or cbseacademic.nic.in).
  2. Manual Verification: Every catalog entry is manually checked for active link availability, correct syllabus edition, and publisher attribution.
  3. Zero User Tracking: StudyHub operates purely on the client side. Your saved bookmarks, daily study planner tasks, and revision checklists exist solely in your own browser's local storage.
  4. Honest Metadata: We do not fabricate user reviews, visitor counts, or popularity claims. Catalog listings reflect actual curriculum availability and honest verification dates.

Distinction from External Providers

StudyHub is strictly a discovery catalog and directory. We are not an examination authority, educational publisher, or coaching institute. We do not set exam papers, declare results, or modify official syllabi. Students are strongly encouraged to verify all deadlines and eligibility rules directly with the relevant statutory body.

Reporting Incorrect or Broken Links

Government websites occasionally restructure URL pathways. If you discover a broken link or misattributed chapter in our catalog, please notify us immediately through our Contact & Corrections Form.
