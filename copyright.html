Intellectual Property & Content Standards

Copyright & Resource Corrections

StudyHub respects the intellectual property rights of educational publishers, statutory examination boards, and content authors.

1. Non-Hosting Educational Indexing Model

StudyHub operates strictly as an educational link directory and search index. It is our policy never to rehost, duplicate, or redistribute proprietary PDF files, textbooks, or paid test papers on our infrastructure.

Every resource card displays the title, publisher attribution, format, and an explicit hyperlink titled "Open Original Resource" that routes the user's browser directly to the publisher's authorized server (such as https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php or https://cbseacademic.nic.in/).

2. Public Domain & Government Information

Official gazetted notifications, public examination blueprints, and government curriculum syllabi published by statutory bodies (such as the Central Board of Secondary Education, National Testing Agency, and Union Public Service Commission) are indexed for public educational benefit and fair nominative reference.

3. Attribution Accuracy & Metadata Corrections

We strive to ensure every indexed resource correctly displays:

  • The legal publisher / authoring organization
  • The applicable academic standard, class, or competitive examination
  • The publication or verification date
  • The applicable license framework (such as NCERT Open Access, Creative Commons, or Official Public Notice)

If you identify an error in chapter titles, edition dates, or source attribution, please notify us using the correction process below.

4. Procedure for Rights Holders to Request Link Modifications

If you are an authorized copyright holder or official statutory representative and wish to request the modification, correction, or delisting of an indexed hyperlink, please submit a written notification containing:

  1. Your full name, official organization, and contact email address.
  2. The specific StudyHub webpage URL and Resource ID where the link is listed.
  3. The original destination URL.
  4. The nature of your request (e.g., updated destination URL, metadata correction, or link removal).

Notifications can be submitted via:

  • Online Form: StudyHub Contact & Link Feedback Form (select "Copyright or Attribution Concern")
  • Direct Email: contact@studyhub.local

Our editorial desk reviews verified requests within 3 to 5 business days and applies catalog adjustments directly.
