How StudyHub Works

An Independent Discovery Index Built for Students

StudyHub acts as an open index for learning materials produced by government examination boards, national educational councils, and authorized open repositories.

  • ✓ No Account or Sign-Up Required: All features work immediately in your browser without logging in.
  • ✓ Direct Official Links: Resources open directly on original domains such as NCERT, CBSE Academic, or NTA.
  • ✓ Local Browser Privacy: Bookmarks, tasks, and revision checklists remain strictly on your personal device.

Resource Verification & Transparency Notice

Every indexed resource undergoes manual verification where we record the original publisher, license status, and verified link target. We do not rehost copyrighted PDFs without authorization.

Important Notice: StudyHub is an independent discovery platform and is not affiliated with NCERT, CBSE, NTA, or UPSC. Official examination schedules and notifications should always be cross-checked on respective official portals.
