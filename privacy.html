Data Privacy Standards

Privacy Policy

An honest, transparent explanation of how data is handled when you use StudyHub.

1. No User Accounts or Server-Side Database

StudyHub is built as a static frontend website. We do not operate user registration systems, logins, password databases, or server-side user profiles. You can search, browse, bookmark, and organize study plans without ever providing personal credentials or identity information.

2. How LocalStorage is Used on Your Device

To make study tools useful across repeat visits without requiring a database, StudyHub utilizes your browser's built-in Web Storage API (LocalStorage):

  • Bookmarks: The resource IDs of items you save are stored under the key studyhub_bookmarks_v1.
  • Recently Viewed: The resource IDs of original links you clicked are stored under studyhub_recent_v1 so you can easily return to them.
  • Study Planner: Your tasks, due dates, and completed checkboxes are stored under studyhub_planner_v1.
  • Revision Checklists: Chapter completion statuses (e.g., in progress, completed) are stored under studyhub_checklists_v1.
  • Theme Preference: Your selected light or dark mode setting is stored under studyhub_theme_v1.

This data exists strictly inside your current browser sandbox on your device. It is never transmitted, synced, or backed up to any StudyHub server. You can delete this data at any time by clearing your browser cache and cookies.

3. Third-Party Web Fonts (Google Fonts)

To maintain typographic clarity across operating systems, StudyHub requests web fonts (Inter and Source Serif 4) from Google Fonts servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When your browser requests these font files, your device's IP address and standard HTTP request headers are received by Google in accordance with Google's public font privacy documentation.

4. External Hyperlinks

StudyHub provides direct links to external websites, including government portals (e.g., ncert.nic.in, cbseacademic.nic.in, nta.ac.in) and open educational repositories. Once you click an external link, you leave StudyHub. External websites maintain their own independent terms of service, server logging practices, and cookie policies over which StudyHub has no control.

5. Contact Forms & Email Communications

When you submit an inquiry through our Contact Form, our website does not send the data to a private server. Instead, it prepares a message directly in your device's default email client via a standard mailto: protocol, or lets you copy the text to your clipboard. Any information you subsequently send via email is governed by your email service provider.

6. No Commercial Advertising or Tracking Pixels

StudyHub does not run behavioral advertising networks, retargeting pixels (such as Meta Pixel), commercial tracking cookies, or fingerprinting scripts.
