Why Timestamp-Based Monotonic Timing Matters

Standard web timers often freeze or drift when you switch tabs because browsers throttle background JavaScript. StudyHub calculates remaining time against an absolute timestamp (Date.now()), so your interval remains exactly on track even if your browser tab is minimized while solving sums on paper.
